📜 Beer-Lambert Principles

Absorbance is directly proportional to concentration ($c$) and optical path length ($l$):

A = ε · c · l

Optimum accuracy occurs between $0.2 \le A \le 1.5\text{ AU}$. High concentrations cause chemical association and non-linear stray light deviations.

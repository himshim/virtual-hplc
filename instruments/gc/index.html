🎓 Gas Chromatography & FID Principles

Master capillary column vapor-phase separation, van Deemter carrier gas efficiency, and Flame Ionization Detection (FID).

📊 Van Deemter Column Efficiency Curve (H vs u)

The Van Deemter equation $H = A + \frac{B}{u} + C \cdot u$ describes plate height ($H$) as a function of linear velocity ($u$). Minimum plate height $H_{\min}$ achieves maximum separation efficiency ($N = L/H$).

1. Column & Temperature Program

Non-polar DB-5 capillary column separates compounds by boiling point and vapor pressure. Higher oven temperature ramps accelerate elution of high boiling point analytes.

2. FID Detector Principle

Eluting hydrocarbons combust in a Hydrogen/Air flame, producing $CHO^+$ ions collected at a polarizing electrode to measure picoampere ($pA$) current proportional to carbon content.

3. Split vs Splitless Injection

Split mode (1:20) prevents column overload for concentrated samples. Splitless mode transfers 100% of sample for trace residual solvent analysis.

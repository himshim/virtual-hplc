💎 ATR Evanescent Wave Penetration Depth (d_p) Calculator

Attenuated Total Reflectance (ATR) uses internal reflection at the crystal-sample interface to produce an evanescent wave that penetrates sample depth $d_p$: d_p = 1 / (2π · ν̃ · n₁ · √(sin²θ - (n₂/n₁)²))

Atmospheric CO₂ Incomplete Subtraction (2349 cm⁻¹)
Appears as an inverted peak or sharp doublet at 2349 cm⁻¹ if room ventilation or breathing changes between background and sample scans. Solution: Re-acquire fresh background.
Water Vapor Rotational Fine Structure (3600–3800 & 1500–1700 cm⁻¹)
Dozens of needle-sharp spikes across the O-H and amide regions caused by atmospheric humidity fluctuations. Solution: Purge bench with dry N₂ or desiccant.

📜 Fourier Transform Math

The interferogram $I(\delta)$ records light intensity as a function of optical path difference $\delta$. Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) converts $I(\delta)$ into a wavenumber spectrum $T(\tilde{\nu})$.

❓ Why Background Scan?

Atmospheric $CO_2$ (2350 cm⁻¹) and water vapor ($H_2O$ 3600 cm⁻¹) absorb IR. Ratioing $T(\tilde{\nu}) = \frac{I_{\text{sample}}}{I_{\text{background}}} \times 100\%$ eliminates atmospheric interference.

📊 ATR vs KBr Sampling

ATR (Diamond) uses evanescent waves (0.5–2 µm depth) for liquids/solids. KBr Pellet provides 1 mm transmission for solids.

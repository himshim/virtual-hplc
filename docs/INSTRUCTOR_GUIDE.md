# 👨‍🏫 Instructor & Faculty Guide (`docs/INSTRUCTOR_GUIDE.md`)

Pedagogical guidance for teaching B.Pharm and M.Pharm practical courses.

---

## 1. Classroom Features
- **Classroom Projection Mode**: Toggle Projection Mode in the top header for 20-foot readability under bright classroom lights.
- **Instructor Control Lock**: Lock input sliders during demonstrations to keep student focus on physical trends.
- **4-Step Diagnosis Framework**: Guide students through:
  1. *Changed Parameter*
  2. *Observed Effect*
  3. *Scientific Reason*
  4. *Suggested Next Step*
